# 📦 Inventory Management & Stock Monitoring Dashboard

An interactive inventory analytics dashboard built using **Google Sheets** to monitor inventory value, stock movement, product performance, warehouse distribution, supplier performance, and stock health.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes **1,000 inventory transactions** with a total inventory value of **₹12.53 Cr**.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of inventory performance across products, categories, warehouses, suppliers, stock status, and monthly stock movement.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Monitor overall inventory value and closing stock
- Identify low-stock, out-of-stock, and overstock items
- Compare closing stock with reorder levels
- Analyze inventory distribution across warehouses and categories
- Identify high-value products
- Monitor monthly stock movement
- Provide interactive filtering for inventory analysis

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Validation

Before building the dashboard, the dataset was cleaned and validated.

Key data preparation steps included:

- Identifying and removing duplicate transactions
- Handling missing category, supplier, and warehouse values
- Standardizing inconsistent product names
- Checking blank and unique values across columns
- Validating stock calculations

### Stock Calculation

Closing Stock = Opening Stock + Stock In - Stock Out + Returned Units - Damaged Units

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### KPI Metrics

- **Total Inventory Value:** ₹12.53 Cr
- **Total Closing Stock:** 94,327
- **Low Stock Items:** 63
- **Out of Stock Items:** 48
- **Overstock Items:** 502

### Visualizations

- Monthly Stock In and Stock Out
- Inventory by Category
- Inventory by Warehouse
- Top 10 Products by Inventory Value
- Closing Stock vs Reorder Level
- Inventory Stock Status

### Interactive Slicers

The dashboard includes interactive filters for:

- Warehouse
- Category
- Stock Status
- Supplier

## 🔎 Key Insights

- **Electronics** represents the largest share of inventory value among the analyzed categories.
- **Bengaluru** has the highest inventory value among the warehouses.
- **502 items** are classified as overstock.
- **63 items** are classified as low stock.
- **48 items** are classified as out of stock.
- The Top 10 analysis highlights products contributing significantly to overall inventory value.
- Closing stock was compared against reorder levels to identify potential inventory risks.
